# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user choice, 2026-11-20): no build step, no dependencies; opens directly in the browser. Design tokens live in CSS custom properties + a JSON source of truth.

## Users

- Primary: iAR internal team — design/development implementing web apps, dashboards and mobile products; marketing building commercial and technical presentations.
- Secondary: external agencies and providers applying the iAR brand under contract.
- Context: B2B industrial and office environments; readers work in Spanish, English and French.

## Product Purpose

A 2027 Corporate Identity Manual + design-system reference for Industrial Augmented Reality S.L. (brand: iAR). It centralizes the visual and verbal identity under the single `iAR` brand, presented in a friendly, approachable way for both non-technical readers and product teams, and standardizes tokens, UI components and slide templates. Success: consistent, accessible application of the iAR brand across products, dashboards, presentations and providers.

## Positioning

iAR: *Applied AI and digital solutions for business and industry*. 15+ years applying technology; accredited Pyme Innovadora; Microsoft Partner (Microsoft 365 + Copilot). Dual reality the identity must serve: industrial plant (reliability, operational clarity) and modern software/AI (clean, current aesthetics).

## Operating Context

- HQ Pamplona (Navarra, Spain), delegation Castres (France); multilingual ES/EN/FR.
- Unified brand architecture: everything is centralized under the single brand `iAR`, paired with clear functional descriptors for solutions (Applied AI, industrial digitalization, continuous improvement, quality inspection, people & operations management, GMAO, traceability, paperless, BI dashboards, cloud Azure/AWS, mobility, AR/VR/MR). No sub-brands are listed or differentiated.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Manual must define (per brand-brief.md §4):
1. Logo: construction, clear space, minimum sizes, versions (primary, monochrome, negative, isotipo for app icons and favicon), coexistence with functional descriptors and partner badges.
2. Palette + design tokens: corporate primary (`#05677f`), accent turquoise (`#22c0b4`), warm complementary (`#d96b27`), neutral scales, semantic colors (success/warning/error/info) with accessible contrast in light and dark mode; explained clearly without overwhelming non-technical readers with raw contrast ratio tables.
3. Typography: Sora for headings, Source Sans 3 for body and UI labels, and IBM Plex Mono restricted to specific technical contexts (code, tokens, drawing dimensions, footers/folios).
4. UI components for apps/dashboards: buttons, inputs, selects, switches, tables, filters, KPI/status cards, progress bars, steppers, breadcrumbs, activity timelines, alerts, modals, navigation.
5. Presentation templates: cover, agenda/sections, comparisons, architecture/flows, metrics/KPIs, use cases, closing — visually engaging and adaptable to commercial and technical proposals.
Deliverables in repo: brand-brief.md (exists), design tokens (colors, type, spacing, radii, shadows/borders), style guide + interactive web manual with real UI and slide examples.

## Product Principles

1. One brand, one system: all solutions communicate under `iAR`, using clear functional descriptors instead of separate sub-brands.
2. Dual-world balance: industrial reliability and modern AI clarity carry equal weight.
3. Multilingual by construction: hierarchy, label lengths and rhythm must survive ES/EN/FR without breakage.
4. Accessibility is a brand rule, not a feature: clear readability and comfortable use in both light and dark modes.
5. The manual demonstrates the system it documents: every rule shown with a real, working example.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The identity must be comfortable to read and easy to use for everyone, in both light and dark modes. Color combinations, text sizes, and interface states are chosen to maintain clear legibility and accessible interaction across screens and documents.
